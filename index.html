Scope

VLA policies only: vision-language-conditioned policies that directly predict actions at inference. World-Action Models (WAM / WRAM) and world-model policies are excluded — GO-3 Nano Preview (AGIBOT WRAM), ViGAR, ME-Dex-1.0, WorldScape Policy 2.0, and the WAM / WM entries (PrismWAM, PICA-WAM, PatchWAM, EWAM, UniWAM, 4D-WAM, X-WAM, FastWAM, AHA-WAM, CloudRobo-WM, MWA-Phoenix). Non-VLA baselines (DP, DP3, ACT, RDT) are excluded from the ranking and listed below as reference only. Methods that use a world model only as a training-time signal but deploy a VLA are kept — e.g. WorldLA, which distils Fast-WAM routing into a π0.5 policy and runs no world model at inference.
